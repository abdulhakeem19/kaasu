## Full description (max 4000)
```
Kaasu is a privacy-first expense tracker for India. It reads your UPI and bank payment notifications on your phone and turns them into a clean spending log — automatically, with no accounts and no cloud.

WHY KAASU
• Private by design — no sign-up, no servers, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your device.
• Automatic — captures debits, credits and UPI payments from the apps you choose, so you barely type anything.
• Honest — open about exactly what it reads (only the payment apps you enable) and what it never touches (your SMS inbox, contacts, location).

WHAT YOU GET
• Auto-captured transactions from GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED and major bank apps, plus bank SMS.
• Smart parsing that ignores promotional "offers" and gets the merchant, amount and debit/credit right.
• Dashboard with monthly spend, this-week trend and daily average.
• Budgets — an overall limit plus per-category limits, with on-pace projection.
• Subscriptions — auto-detects recurring payments, shows renewal dates and what you spend; add your own.
• "Needs a tag" review queue to categorize quickly, with merchant auto-tag rules.
• Accounts auto-created from your bank/card, with a per-account view.
• Reports — category breakdown donut, monthly heatmap and CSV export.
• App lock — PIN with optional fingerprint, plus hide-amounts-on-lock.
• Full backup & restore to a local file you control.

YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE
Kaasu never uploads your transactions. There are no accounts to hack and no cloud to breach. Back up or export your data as a file whenever you want, and wipe everything with one tap.

PERMISSIONS
• Notification access — to read payment alerts from the apps you enable (on-device only).
• Notifications (optional) — for budget and renewal reminders you turn on.
Kaasu does not request SMS, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Made in Chennai. Built for people who want to understand their spending without handing their financial life to a server.
```
