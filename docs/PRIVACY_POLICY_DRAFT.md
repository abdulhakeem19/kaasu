## Privacy Policy for Kaasu

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

Kaasu is a private expense tracker designed to help users understand their spending from transaction notifications. This Privacy Policy explains what data Kaasu accesses, how it is used, and how users can control their data.

## 1. What Kaasu Does

Kaasu helps users track expenses by detecting transaction alerts from UPI, bank, wallet, and card notifications. The app organizes spending into categories and shows dashboards and reports.

Kaasu is not a banking app, payment app, lending app, credit score app, investment app, or financial advisory service.

## 2. Data Kaasu Accesses

Kaasu may access the following data on your device:

- Transaction notification text from supported payment, bank, wallet, and card apps
- Transaction amount
- Merchant or sender/receiver name when available
- Transaction type such as expense, income, refund, or transfer
- Notification source app name/package
- Transaction date and time
- Categories, notes, rules, and budgets created by you
- Manually added expense data

## 3. Notification Access

Kaasu uses Android Notification Access to detect transaction alerts automatically.

Notification Access is used only to identify transaction-related alerts and create expense records. Kaasu is designed to ignore unrelated notifications whenever possible.

You can disable notification access anytime from Android Settings.

## 4. Local Storage

In the MVP version, Kaasu stores transaction data locally on your device.

Kaasu does not require an account, bank login, or cloud sync for the MVP.

## 5. Data Kaasu Does Not Collect in MVP

Kaasu does not collect or request:

- Bank login credentials
- UPI PIN
- Card PIN
- OTPs
- SMS permission
- Call logs
- Contacts
- Location
- Camera access
- Microphone access

## 6. Data Sharing

In the MVP version, Kaasu does not sell your data and does not share your transaction data with third parties.

If future versions add optional cloud backup, analytics, crash reporting, or AI features, this policy must be updated before release and users must be clearly informed.

## 7. Ads, Loans, and Financial Offers

Kaasu does not show ads in the MVP.

Kaasu does not provide loan offers, credit card offers, insurance offers, or lending recommendations in the MVP.

## 8. Data Export

Kaasu may allow you to export your transaction data as CSV or another local file format.

You are responsible for storing or sharing exported files safely.

## 9. Data Deletion

You can delete your data from inside the app.

When you use the delete-all-data option, Kaasu deletes locally stored transactions, categories, rules, notes, budgets, and settings from the app database, except any files you previously exported and saved outside the app.

## 10. Security

Kaasu is designed to minimize data exposure by processing and storing data locally on your device.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. You should protect your device with a screen lock and keep your Android system updated.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

Kaasu is not designed for children. If the app is made available to younger users, the Play Store rating, privacy practices, and parental requirements must be reviewed carefully.

## 12. Financial Disclaimer

Kaasu is a tracking and budgeting utility. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, legal, lending, or banking advice.

Users should verify transaction data manually when accuracy is important.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted within the app or on the official Kaasu privacy policy page.
